Star Customs Rules

Our rules help keep Star Customs professional, welcoming, and safe for everyone.

01

Staff Rules

  • Staff must treat customers, members, and other staff respectfully.
  • Staff may not abuse permissions or use their position for personal benefit.
  • Customer information, private conversations, and unreleased designs must remain confidential.
  • Staff may not steal, resell, copy, or redistribute another designer's work.
  • Staff should communicate professionally and provide accurate information.
  • Staff must follow management instructions and the procedures of the server.
  • Conflicts with customers should be escalated to management rather than becoming public arguments.
  • Staff actions may be reviewed by management when necessary.
  • Serious violations may result in removal from the Star Customs staff team.
02

Member Rules

  • Be respectful toward staff and other community members.
  • No harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
  • No scams, impersonation, or attempts to deceive other members.
  • Do not steal, trace, redistribute, or claim ownership of Star Customs designs without permission.
  • Do not spam, advertise, raid, or disrupt the Discord server.
  • Orders and payments must follow the instructions provided by Star Customs staff.
  • Do not submit fraudulent payment claims or intentionally abuse chargebacks.
  • Use the correct channels and follow staff instructions when requesting support.
  • Members must comply with Discord and Roblox rules in addition to these rules.
Enforcement

Star Customs may remove content, restrict access, suspend members, or take other reasonable action when rules are violated. Staff may consider the context and severity of a situation when enforcing these standards.
